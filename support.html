Common questions

Where are the settings?

Open the Profile tab and tap the gear icon, Settings.

How do I get Nurtail Plus, or restore it on a new phone?

Open Settings and look for the Nurtail Plus section. Tap See Nurtail Plus to choose a plan, or Restore purchases to get back a plan you already bought with the same Apple Account. Plus adds more than one pet, the vet report PDF and the care sheet PDF. Everything else is free.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Nurtail Plus and cancel. Apple manages all subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

My reminders do not arrive

  1. In Nurtail, open Settings and check that Care reminders is on.
  2. Tap Open iOS Settings and make sure notifications are allowed for Nurtail.
  3. Check that no Focus mode is silencing Nurtail.

How do I back up my pet's records or move them to a new phone?

In Settings, open Backup and restore and tap Back up data. Save the file in Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install Nurtail and tap Restore from backup. Your normal iPhone backup also contains the app's data.

How do I add the widget?

Press and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap the plus button, search for Nurtail and add Today's care. It also comes as a Lock Screen widget: press and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize and add it.

How do I switch the anonymous usage statistics on or off?

In Settings, use the Share anonymous usage switch. See the privacy policy for what it sends.

How do I erase everything?

In Settings, tap Erase all data.... This removes your pets and records from the phone and cannot be undone, so make a backup first if you want to keep them.

Does Nurtail give medical advice?

No. Nurtail keeps records and reminders. If your pet is unwell, call your vet.
