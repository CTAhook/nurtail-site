Privacy Policy

Nurtail: Pet Health Tracker · Last updated 4 October 2026

The short version

  • You do not need an account. Nurtail does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your pet's records, photos, reminders and notes are stored on your phone. I do not receive them.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are sent only if you say yes on the "Help improve Nurtail" card or switch on "Share anonymous usage" in Settings. Until then the analytics service is not started and the app does not contact it.
  • There are no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and I do not sell data.
  • Payments for Nurtail Plus are handled by Apple.

Who is responsible

Nurtail is made by Nabil Ahmindach, an individual developer in Belgium. For the optional usage statistics described below I am the data controller under the GDPR. You can reach me at nabil-ahmindach@hotmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Pet profiles, check-ins, logs, photos you attach to a log, reminders, vet and caretaker details, weights and other measurements, and your settings are saved inside the app on your phone. I cannot see any of it. The home-screen and lock-screen widget reads a copy of today's care that the app keeps on the same phone, in storage shared by the app and its widget.

Your phone's passcode and encryption protect this data. If you use iCloud Backup or another device backup, Apple handles that copy under your Apple settings. A backup you create with "Back up data" is a file you save where you choose, for example in Files or iCloud Drive. Vet reports and care sheets are PDFs that leave your phone only when you share them.

Deleting the app removes its data from your phone. "Erase all data" in Settings does the same inside the app.

Notifications, photos and files

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your phone. They do not use a server. iOS asks for your permission when you save your first reminder, and you can change it in iOS Settings. Nurtail uses a photo only when you add one to a log, and a file only when you choose it to restore a backup. Nothing is uploaded.

Optional anonymous usage statistics

On the first screen Nurtail asks whether you want to help improve it by sharing anonymous usage. Nothing is shared unless you switch it on, and you can change your mind at any time in Settings under "Share anonymous usage". While it is off, the analytics service is not started. When you switch it off, the app stops it and sends nothing more.

If you switch it on, the app sends the following to PostHog, a product analytics service that works for me as my processor. I use PostHog Cloud EU, which stores data on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.

  • Which welcome and setup screens you reach, whether you finish setup, and what kind of pet you set up (for example dog or cat). Never its name.
  • Whether you see, close or use the Nurtail Plus offer, which Plus feature you tried to open, and whether a trial or purchase started or failed (the plan, not your payment details).
  • That you created your first log (and its type, for example a weight) or your first reminder, and that you completed a daily check-in. Never what you wrote.
  • When the app is installed, updated, opened or sent to the background.
  • Technical details that the analytics library adds to every event: app version, iOS version, device model (for example iPhone), screen size, whether the connection is Wi-Fi or cellular, your language and time zone, and random IDs that the library creates: one for this installation and one for each time you use the app. The IDs are not your name, your Apple Account or your phone's advertising ID. They let me count how many installations do something. The installation ID stays until you delete the app.

The app may also ask PostHog which version of a screen to show, so that I can test two versions against each other.

The statistics never include your pet's name, breed, notes, weights, photos, vet details or anything else you type. I do not record your screen, I do not capture your taps, and I do not identify you by name.

I have asked PostHog not to store IP addresses, and the app marks every event it sends so that PostHog does not look up a location from your connection. Your time zone, which is one of the technical details above, still gives a rough idea of your region.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by switching the setting off. Withdrawing does not affect what was sent before. Retention: I keep these statistics for as long as they help me improve Nurtail, and then delete them.

Purchases

Nurtail Plus is an optional subscription sold by Apple. Apple takes the payment and manages the subscription. I do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks on your phone, through Apple's StoreKit, whether you have an active plan. Apple tells me anonymous totals, such as the number of subscribers. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. If you allow sharing with app developers in your iPhone's analytics settings, Apple may also give me anonymous statistics about downloads and crashes.

Children

Nurtail is for pet owners and is not aimed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict your personal data, to receive it in a usable format, to object to its use, and to withdraw your consent. The usage statistics are not linked to your name or account, so I usually cannot tell which data is yours. The simplest way to stop is to switch off "Share anonymous usage" in Settings. For any request or question, write to nabil-ahmindach@hotmail.com. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

Changes to this policy

If I change what the app does with data, I will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.
