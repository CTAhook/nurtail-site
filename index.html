Nurtail: Pet Health Tracker

The pet health tracker for dogs, cats, rabbits and other pets. Log symptoms, keep vaccine and medication records, and get reminders so nothing is missed. No account needed.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

What you can do

  • Keep pet health records in one place: vaccines, medication, vet visits and conditions such as allergies, injuries and recoveries.
  • Never miss a dose: reminders for medication, vaccines, vet-visit follow-ups, meals and walks. Mark them done or snooze them right from the notification.
  • Check in daily in seconds: eating, drinking, energy and more, one tap each. Green is normal, orange means something changed.
  • Spot changes early: 7-day trends, a weight chart with the change over time, and key insights such as your streak and the next vaccine.
  • Keep your vet one tap away: save the clinic and call from your pet's profile.
  • See today's care at a glance with the home-screen and lock-screen widget.
  • Support every pet: dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, fish, reptiles and others, each with its own daily signals.

Nurtail Plus

Nurtail is free for one pet. Nurtail Plus is an optional subscription that adds more than one pet, the vet report PDF (pet details, active conditions, vaccine status and weight trend, ready to share with your vet) and the care sheet PDF for sitters and family. Choose monthly or yearly; the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

Private by design

No account, and your pet's records stay on your phone. Optional anonymous usage statistics are off until you agree. Read the privacy policy.

Nurtail keeps records and reminders. It does not give medical advice or replace your vet.
